Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Thyroxeia AI uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Cookies and Local Storage Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a site keep small amounts of data on your device. Both are used to remember things between page loads.

2. What We Use

We use only what is necessary to run the service:

  • Sign-in session storage — keeps you logged in between visits (provided by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Preference storage — remembers settings such as your chosen study mode and locally saved weak-card tracking.
  • Security storage — protects against unauthorised requests and helps keep your account safe.

3. What We Do NOT Use

  • No advertising or marketing cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking or behavioural profiling.
  • No third-party analytics pixels that identify you.
  • No sale of cookie data to anyone.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features load components from third parties (for example, PayPal at checkout, and Google Fonts for typography). Those providers may set their own cookies when you interact with their components, governed by their own policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Note that blocking essential storage will sign you out and may prevent the app from working properly. All major browsers let you:

  • view and delete stored cookies and site data;
  • block third-party cookies;
  • use private/incognito mode, which clears data when you close the window.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect the latest revision.
