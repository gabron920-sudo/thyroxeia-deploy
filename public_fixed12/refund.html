Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how subscriptions to Thyroxeia AI are billed, cancelled, and refunded. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Free Plan

You can use Thyroxeia AI on the free plan at no cost. No payment is required to create an account or to use the free features.

2. Subscription Billing

  • Premium plans are billed through PayPal on a recurring basis (monthly unless stated otherwise at checkout).
  • The price, billing period, and currency are shown to you before you confirm payment.
  • By confirming payment, the adult account holder (parent or legal guardian) authorises the recurring charge until cancellation.

3. Cancelling Your Subscription

  • You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals.
  • You keep premium access until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • To cancel: open the subscription in your PayPal account and stop the recurring payment, or email rocafortronin@gmail.com and we will help.

4. Refund Policy

All sales are final. We do not provide refunds or credits for the current billing period, for unused time, or for partial periods, except where a refund is required by applicable law or by PayPal's own buyer protection rules.

Because Thyroxeia AI delivers digital access immediately upon purchase, the right to withdraw from a distance contract does not apply once premium access has begun, to the extent permitted by law.

5. Exceptions

We may, at our sole discretion, issue a refund or credit in cases such as:

  • a duplicate or accidental charge for the same period;
  • a charge made without the account holder's authorisation;
  • a technical fault on our side that prevented access for a significant part of the paid period.

Requests must be sent to rocafortronin@gmail.com within 14 days of the charge, with the PayPal transaction reference.

6. Failed or Disputed Payments

If a payment fails, premium access may be suspended until it is resolved. If you open a chargeback or dispute instead of contacting us first, we may suspend the account while the dispute is reviewed.

7. Price Changes

We may change subscription prices. Any change applies to future billing periods and will be shown before you are charged. You may cancel before the new price takes effect.

8. Contact

Billing and refund questions: rocafortronin@gmail.com
