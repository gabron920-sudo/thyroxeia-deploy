Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Thyroxeia AI is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially student learners under 18. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the rights available to you and your parent or guardian under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Who We Are

Thyroxeia AI is an educational study platform operated by a student founder under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. For privacy matters, the contact point is rocafortronin@gmail.com.

2. Data Minimisation — What We Collect

We deliberately collect as little as possible in order to protect student users. We collect:

  • Account data: an email address and password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Learning data: the decks, flashcards, and study progress you create, plus study statistics such as streaks and cards reviewed.
  • Subscription status: which plan you are on (free or premium), and confirmation identifiers from PayPal.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information and error logs needed to keep the service working and secure.

We do not require or intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as real full names, home addresses, phone numbers, school IDs, government IDs, biometric data, or health information. Please do not submit these to the service.

3. Children's Privacy and Parental Consent

  • Thyroxeia AI is intended for K-12 students. If a user is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must consent to the use of the service and to any purchase.
  • If a user is under 13, we require verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect more data from a child than is reasonably necessary to provide the learning service.
  • A parent or legal guardian may contact us at any time to review their child's data, request correction, or request full deletion. We will act on such requests promptly.
  • We do not use children's data for advertising, profiling, or sale of any kind.

4. Financial and Payment Data

All premium subscription payments are processed securely by PayPal. Thyroxeia AI does not collect, view, or store credit card numbers, bank account details, or financial passwords. Payment data is handled entirely by PayPal under its own security infrastructure and privacy policy. We receive only limited confirmation information, such as whether a payment succeeded and the subscription status.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your study preferences. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party marketing pixels. See our Cookie Policy for details on how to clear or block these.

6. How We Use Your Data

  • to create and manage your account and sign you in;
  • to save and display your decks, progress, and study statistics;
  • to generate AI study material you request;
  • to process subscriptions and confirm payment status;
  • to send essential service emails such as verification codes and password resets;
  • to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose errors;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

We rely on your consent (and, for minors, your guardian's consent) and on the necessity of processing to provide the service you requested.

7. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We share data only with processors that are necessary to run the service, and only to the extent needed:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting;
  • Google Gemini — AI generation of study material from your prompts;
  • PayPal — payment processing;
  • Email delivery provider — sending verification and account emails;
  • Hosting/CDN providers — serving the website and backend.

We may also disclose data where required by law, legal process, or a lawful request from a competent authority, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

8. Data Retention

We keep account and learning data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we erase your account and associated learning data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain limited records (for example, payment or tax records) by law. Aggregated, non-identifying statistics may be retained.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, access-controlled database infrastructure, and hashed password storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable and appropriate organisational, physical, and technical measures to protect your data.

10. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers located outside the Philippines. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data continues to receive a comparable level of protection.

11. Your Rights Under RA 10173

Subject to the Data Privacy Act, you (or your parent or guardian on your behalf) have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is processed;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • object to processing, or request suspension of processing;
  • request erasure or blocking of your data;
  • request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

To exercise any of these rights, email rocafortronin@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and in accordance with the Act.

12. Breach Notification

If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users and the National Privacy Commission in accordance with the Data Privacy Act and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Privacy questions, data access, and deletion requests: rocafortronin@gmail.com

This document is a plain-language template provided for convenience and does not constitute legal advice. For high-value or regulated matters, consult a licensed Philippine data protection practitioner.
