Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Thyroxeia AI is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially student learners under 18. This policy explains how we collect and handle data in compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

1. Data Minimization (What We Collect)

To maximize student safety, we practice strict data minimization. We only collect:

  • Account credentials: an email address and password created by the user to save quiz scores and learning progress.
  • Learning data: decks, flashcards, and study progress you create in the app.

We do not require or store sensitive personal information such as real full names, home addresses, phone numbers, or school IDs.

2. Financial and Payment Data

All premium subscription payments are processed securely through PayPal. Thyroxeia AI does not collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial passwords. Your payment data is handled entirely by PayPal under their secure infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use account information strictly to manage user logins and display personalized learning progress. We will never sell, rent, or share student data with third-party advertisers or external companies.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to protect account data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Authentication data is stored with our backend provider using encrypted, access-controlled infrastructure.

5. Children's Privacy

If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must consent to your use of the platform. We minimize the data we collect for exactly this reason, and we do not knowingly collect more than is necessary to operate the learning service.

6. Your Rights and Account Deletion

Under the Data Privacy Act, you (or your parent/guardian) have the right to review, update, or permanently delete your account data. To erase your account and all associated progress data from our servers, email us at the address below.

7. Third-Party Services

We use trusted providers strictly to operate the service: payment processing (PayPal), authentication and database hosting, AI generation (Google Gemini), and email delivery. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, email: rocafortronin@gmail.com
